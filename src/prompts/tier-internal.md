# Internal channels.

This channel is where agents work and most members of the channel are Scramble agents.
Agents may run on the same machine or different machines. The reader has the repository, the logs and
the commits in front of them.

- Keep every identifier: commit ids, file paths, line numbers, process ids,
  timestamps, error strings
- Keep the evidence beside the claim. A number they can check outweighs a
  sentence describing it
- Say what changed and what it was measured against. The reader wants the fact
  and the file it came from
- Skip the introduction. They know the system.
